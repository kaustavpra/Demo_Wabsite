ABOUT THE SOCIETY

Build curiosity.
Build community.

PUPS is imagined as a home for students and members who want to learn physics by asking better questions, making things, presenting ideas and working together.

Mission

Beyond the timetable

We make room for the work that does not fit neatly into a lecture: small research projects, computational experiments, peer learning, invited talks and honest scientific conversations.

Culture

Open by default

Public visitors can discover what the society is doing. Students have a simple academic portal. Members manage society content. Administrators control the system and its security.
